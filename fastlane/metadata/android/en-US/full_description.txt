Battery Chan turns battery milestones into personal pop-up alerts, with your own image, sound and message for each one.

<b>Custom events</b>
Create your own events at any battery level from 1 to 100%. For example: "at 80% while charging, play this sound and show this picture". Each event can fire while charging up, while draining, or both. Edit, disable or delete them any time.

<b>Built-in events</b>
- Low battery (20%)
- Charger connected
- Full charge (100%)
- Charger unplugged

<b>Battery details</b>
- Live voltage, energy (Wh / Ah) and temperature
- "Charging since" and time-to-full estimate
- Optional live reading in the status bar (V, Wh, Ah, °C or %)

<b>Look and feel</b>
- Material 3 design with Material You dynamic color on Android 12+
- System, Light, Dark and pure black (AMOLED) themes

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no analytics, no accounts and no network access. Your images and sounds are copied into the app's private storage and never leave your device.

<b>Permissions</b>
- Notifications: event alerts and the monitor notification
- Display over other apps: show the pop-up on top of whatever you are doing
- Ignore battery optimization: keep the monitor alive in the background

Parts of the battery metrics engine are adapted from Wattz (MIT) by dubrowgn.
